# S2 and G2 photo reconstruction

Editable Blender sources: s2.blend, g2.blend. Browser exports: s2.glb, g2.glb. Generator: build-devices.py.

Reconstructed from the user-supplied Genstar S2.png, Genstar S2 2.png and 设备正面应用UI (1).png. These are visual exhibition models, not manufacturer CAD and not verified engineering dimensions. Counter device positions, rotations and quantities remain in DEVICE_PLACEMENTS.

Each model exposes separately named screen meshes. Browser content is painted onto these screens with the selected locale; it is not baked into the product housing. Main, customer and rear screens retain independent display roles.

# Food imagery

Soft directional light, natural texture, warm highlights. No objects crossing quadrant boundaries.
